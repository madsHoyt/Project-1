Ocean's Ember Jar

Wheel-thrown ceramic jar with a fitted lid, combining timeless craftsmanship with a striking interplay of colors.

  • LBM clay fired to cone 10 with 2 coats of blue celadón - This high-fire process ensures maximum durability, resulting in a strong, vitrified ceramic body. The blue celadón glaze, applied in two layers, deepens the color and enhances the piece's smooth, glossy finish.
  • Tints of orange underglaze shine through the blue glaze, capturing beautiful undertones - The subtle warmth of the orange underglaze peeks through the celadón, creating a dynamic contrast that shifts with lighting and angle. This unique effect gives the jar a captivating depth and one-of-a-kind character.
  • Thoughtfully designed lid for both aesthetic appeal and function - The well-fitted lid complements the jar's form, ensuring a seamless silhouette while providing practical usability for storage or display.
  • Hand-thrown for a truly unique, artisanal touch - The jar carries the natural variations of wheel-thrown pottery, making the piece a one-of-a-kind work of art.
